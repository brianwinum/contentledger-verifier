What this result does not establish

Ownership, authorship, human identity, exclusive key custody, first publication, or the truth of the content. It also does not establish the latest website state, completeness beyond this package, trusted time, Bitcoin consensus, or current archive availability.

Original article text is not reproduced or independently checked against its content digest. Timestamp proofs, when present, are checked for structure only; archive references are retained metadata.
