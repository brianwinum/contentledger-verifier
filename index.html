HOW IT WORKS

Understand the evidence
behind the package.

The checker examines the package's contents, carried signatures, and identity history, where present.

  1. 01

    Choose a package

    Use the original ZIP you received.

  2. 02

    Run the offline checks

    No account or internet connection needed.

  3. 03

    Read the result

    See what passed and any limitations.

Have an Identity Recovery Pack?

Keep it private. This checker accepts public evidence bundles only; it cannot check recovery packs or private keys.

CHECK COMPLETE

Reading the package and checking its evidence. Larger packages may take a moment.

What this result does not establish

Ownership, authorship, human identity, exclusive key custody, first publication, or the truth of the content. It also does not establish the latest website state, completeness beyond this package, trusted time, Bitcoin consensus, or current archive availability.

Original article text is not reproduced or independently checked against its content digest. Timestamp proofs, when present, are checked for structure only; archive references are retained metadata.

Keep a record of this check

Save a local summary with the result and package fingerprint. Filenames, paths, comparison values, and raw evidence details are omitted. This is not a signed attestation.
